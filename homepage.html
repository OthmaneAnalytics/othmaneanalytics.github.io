Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Laboratoire de Probabilités, Statistique et Modélisation, Sorbonne University and Université Paris Cité, from 2023 to 2026.

Research interests

I develop mathematical and statistical foundations for learning and generating stochastic systems from dependent data, with particular emphasis on generative modelling, optimal transport, stochastic control, and PDE methods.

My work combines finite-sample statistical guarantees, transport and control formulations for stochastic generative models, and analytical methods for reliable scientific machine learning.
